Engineering details for Affirmation App

I used Clean Architecture to share business logic across Android and iOS with Kotlin Multiplatform and build the interface with Compose Multiplatform. The app uses Coroutines and Flow for asynchronous work and state, Ktor for networking, Koin for dependency injection and Room for local caching. The shared architecture supports media playback, reminders and content management.

Supabase uses JWT authentication. Edge Functions authorise voice sessions, manage their lifecycle and enforce usage quotas. PostgreSQL holds conversation and session records with Row-Level Security (RLS).

Real-time voice was implemented and tested on Android and iOS during development.
