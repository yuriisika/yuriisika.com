FAQ

A few answers.

Can I download AffirmationApp today?

AffirmationApp is an internal working prototype. A public release date has yet to be announced.

Which platforms are in development?

Android and iOS share a Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform foundation, with Supabase supporting the backend.

What can I try in the prototype?

The internal build includes a mood check-in, AI conversations, affirmations you can read or listen to, narrated meditations and sound playlists.

How does the AI companion work?

It offers text conversations and timed interactive voice sessions for adults who want to reflect on everyday thoughts.

Is this a medical or crisis service?

AffirmationApp is for self-guided everyday wellbeing. It does not provide diagnosis, therapy, medical treatment or emergency crisis support.

Are subscriptions available now?

There is no live subscription or checkout. The prices shown here illustrate the proposed UK launch model.

How would Premium and Voice Packs differ?

Premium would expand content and AI text access. Separate Voice Packs would provide metered interactive voice time. Allowances and terms remain undecided.
